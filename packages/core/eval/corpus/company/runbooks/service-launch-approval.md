## Approval Path {#approval}

A director-level approver signs the launch request once the owning team confirms the [production readiness checklist](../standards/production-readiness.md) is complete.

## Emergency Exceptions {#exceptions}

An incomplete checklist may still launch with explicit, written risk acceptance from the approver, logged in the launch request itself.
