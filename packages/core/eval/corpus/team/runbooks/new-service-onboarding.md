## Repository Setup {#repo}

Create the service repository from the team template and wire it into the standard CI pipeline before writing any business logic.

## Meeting the Bar {#bar}

Work through the [production readiness checklist](../standards/production-readiness.md) before requesting a production namespace. A service that skips it does not get one.

## Adding to the Rotation {#rotation}

Once live, add the service to the team's alert rotation and link its dashboard from the team index.
