## Before You Ship {#before}

Run through this checklist before a new service ships to production for the first time. It is meant to be read top to bottom in one sitting, not skimmed.

## Checklist Items {#items}

Confirm the health endpoint responds, confirm alerts are wired to the on-call rotation, confirm the dashboard exists, and confirm a rollback has been tested end to end.

## Tuesday Launch Review {#review}

New services must be presented at the Tuesday Launch Review meeting before they ship. Bring the dashboard link and the rollback plan; the review is skipped only with director sign-off.
